# FRAME/12 Trademark Policy

The FRAME/12 **code** is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You can use, copy, modify, fork, redistribute, and sell it under that license's terms. Nothing in this policy restricts anything the MIT License allows.

This policy covers something the MIT License does not: the **name and identity** of the project. Its goal is simple: people should always be able to tell the official FRAME/12 project apart from forks and derivative work.

## The marks

- The name **FRAME/12™** (also written FRAME12 or Frame/12)
- The FRAME/12 logo and icon (for example [`site/app/icon.svg`](site/app/icon.svg))
- The domain **frame12.dev**

These are unregistered trademarks of Laqwan Brodie.

## Always fine, no permission needed

- **Saying what you use or build on.** For example: "Built with FRAME/12", "FRAME/12-compatible", "A fork of FRAME/12", or "Based on FRAME/12 by Laqwan Brodie".
- **Writing about FRAME/12.** Articles, tutorials, talks, videos, reviews, comparisons, and course material.
- **Linking** to the repository or to frame12.dev.
- **Redistributing unmodified FRAME/12 code** under its own name, with the MIT copyright notice kept intact.

## Please don't

- Name a fork, product, package, registry, template, or service **"FRAME/12"**, or something confusingly similar such as "FRAME/12 Pro" or "Frame12 UI".
- Suggest that your project is **official**, or that it is **endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with** FRAME/12 when it isn't.
- Use the FRAME/12 **logo or icon** for modified versions or for your own products.
- Register **domain names, package names, registry namespaces, or social handles** that could pass as official FRAME/12 channels.

## If you fork FRAME/12

1. **Give your fork its own name and visual identity.**
2. You're welcome to say it is **"based on FRAME/12"** or **"a fork of FRAME/12"**.
3. **Keep the MIT copyright and permission notice** in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), as the license requires.

## Official sources

FRAME/12 is only published at:

- **Source:** [github.com/lbrodie11/frame12](https://github.com/lbrodie11/frame12)
- **Docs and showcase:** [frame12.dev](https://frame12.dev)
- **shadcn registry:** `https://frame12.dev/r/<name>.json`

Anything else claiming to be FRAME/12 is unofficial.

## Questions, permission, or reporting misuse

Open an issue or discussion on [the repository](https://github.com/lbrodie11/frame12). Requests that fall outside this policy are often fine; just ask.

This policy may be updated as the project grows. It is not legal advice.
